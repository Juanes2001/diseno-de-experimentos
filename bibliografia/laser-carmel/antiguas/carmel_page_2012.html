Carmel - Compact Fiber Based Femtosecond Laser (CFL)

  • Overview
  • Specifications
  • Related Products

The Carmel fiber based femtosecond lasers (CFL series) offers high power (500mW at 780nm), ultra-short pulses (<0.3 ps @ 50 MHz), and stable performance for industrial, scientific, biomedical applications at a fraction of the price, size, and cost of operation of traditional Ti:Sapphire laser systems.

The Carmel CFL-05RFF0 is a high power, 780 nm, air cooled, fiber based femtosecond laser with an ultra compact laser head, about 300 times smaller than many Ti:Sapphire lasers with a similar output power levels. It is an easy to use, rugged, industrial laser system.

The Carmel’s operation is based upon the company’s proprietary is a passively mode-locked fiber laser technology, utilizing a saturable absorber, to ensure reliable startup and stable long term performance, with hands free operation. The Carmel offers user-friendly front panel control knobs for flexible system adjustment. The pulse width is less than 300 fs with a negligible pedestal. The timing jitter is less than 60 fs. The pulse energy is greater than 10 nJ. The repetition rate can be specified from 10 to 50 MHz with a polarization-maintaining (PM) output and excellent beam quality, with an M2 < 1.15. An RF synchronization output is provided as a trigger signal.

The Carmel high power, fiber based femtosecond lasers enable you to focus on the job at hand, and not the laser tool being used.
